About

The Biggest ICT Event
In the History of Tangail District

Bindubasini Boys' Computer Club presents the inaugural ICT Fest a landmark event uniting students, educators, and innovators from across Tangail for a celebration of technology and creativity.

With five distinct competitions ICT Quiz, Science Project Showdown, Coding Competition, Chess, Rubik's Cube plus distinguished guests and school exhibitions, this is the largest technology festival the district has ever witnessed.

Your Journey to the
Grand Finale

From registration to awards here's how ICT Fest 2026 unfolds.

1
Step 01 Register

Registration Opens

Schools register for the Science Project Showdown (2 students + 1 teacher per team). Individuals sign up for the Coding Competition. Quiz and all participants can register on the spot.

2
Step 02 Qualify

Selection Rounds

Online screening for Science Project and Coding events. Judges evaluate submissions and select the top 10 teams (Science) and top 5 individuals (Coding) to advance to the main event.

3
Step 03 Fest Day

Fest Day Grand Event

A full day at Bindubasini Boys' School: live quiz, science project exhibitions at school stalls, coding finale, chess, rubik's cube, special guest sessions, and cultural programs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Students from class 6-10 can participate. The ICT Quiz is open to all individuals. The Science Project Showdown requires school-based teams (2 students + 1 teacher). The Coding Competition is open to individual participants.

For the Quiz section the fee is 19 BDT, for the Coding Competition the fee is 49 BDT, and the other sections are free.

For the Science Project Showdown and Coding Competition, online screening rounds will be conducted. Top 10 teams (Science) and top 5 individuals (Coding) advance to fest day. The ICT Quiz is held live with no prior selection.

Selected teams can bring a powerpoint presentation or any presentation on a pen drive, science project or any mean, with which they can showcase their idea or project.

Selected participants will be notified. Specific guidelines and problem formats will be shared with selected finalists before the fest.

Schools register through the official form for the Science Project Showdown (2 students + 1 teacher). Students can register individually for other events. The registration link is available on the website and our Facebook page. Participants can also register via physical forms.
